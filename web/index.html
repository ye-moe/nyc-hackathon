A Way Home,
For Every Voucher

Homeward helps New Yorkers who are homeless find housing that will actually take them. We catch landlords who illegally refuse CityFHEPS, surface affordable vacancies, and match people to the shelters they're eligible for tonight.

How it works Start a check

About 65,000 households hold a CityFHEPS voucher, yet many stay in shelter for months because landlords won't take it. Refusing a voucher is illegal in NYC. Homeward makes that visible, reportable, and easier to get past.

How it works

From listing to action

Collect

A scraper gathers rental listings every day. Caseworkers can also paste a listing or scan a flyer with their phone.

Check

Rules and AI read each listing in any language, find refusals and income rules applied to the full rent, and show the math.

Act

Clean listings and lotteries go to Open Doors. Flagged ones get an evidence packet and a complaint draft a person can review and send.
