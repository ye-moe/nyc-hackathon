Voucher Guard · field scanner

Scan a listing on the spot

Point the camera at a flyer, a lobby sign, or a printed listing. This reads the text (in any language) and checks it for illegal source-of-income discrimination: refusing vouchers or programs, or income requirements that mathematically exclude CityFHEPS holders. It shows the math and drafts a complaint for a person to review.
